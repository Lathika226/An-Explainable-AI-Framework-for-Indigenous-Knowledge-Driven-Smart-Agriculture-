# IEEE Research Alignment

The application is the implementation/testbed for the research framework.

Core research contribution:

**Explainable + indigenous knowledge-driven agricultural decision support**

Primary research modules:

1. XGBoost crop recommendation
2. SHAP explainability
3. Indigenous Knowledge RAG
4. Voice/multilingual accessibility
5. Weather-aware continuous crop monitoring
6. CNN + Grad-CAM plant health analysis

For the paper, evaluate actual models using documented datasets, train/validation/test splits, cross-validation where appropriate, baseline comparisons, and reproducible metrics.

Do not treat React, login, dashboard design or PostgreSQL as the research contribution.
